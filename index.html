welcome to TripTree

Stay Close to the Wild

TripTree brings together nature stays on the edges of South India's best-known forests, Bandipur, Mudumalai and Masinagudi, along with a quiet retreat near the Harangi backwaters in Coorg. Our properties are small and personal, offering warm, home-style hospitality and plenty of space to slow down.

Spend your days on a jungle safari, walking through the countryside or watching deer graze at sunset, and your evenings around a bonfire.

We Invite You To
Celebrate Wildlife

Our Bandipur, Masinagudi and Mudumalai properties sit close to some of the richest wildlife habitats in the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve. Herds of spotted deer, gaur and a wide variety of birds are regular visitors, and an unexpected encounter at your doorstep is all part of the experience.
Jungle safaris are run by the Forest Department at an additional charge, payable on arrival. We're happy to help you plan yours.
