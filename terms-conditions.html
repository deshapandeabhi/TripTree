Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to all bookings made with TripTree Resorts & Homestays.
Please read them before confirming your stay. If anything is unclear, call us on +91 99000 05639 or email booking@triptree.co.in.
  • Bookings and confirmation
    A booking is confirmed only once you receive a confirmation from TripTree by phone, email or message, along with any advance payment requested. Rates shown on this website are indicative and may vary by season, occupancy and availability; the rate stated in your confirmation applies.
  • Payments
    Payment details, including any advance required, are shared with your booking confirmation. Charges for extras such as additional meals, extra beds and activities are payable at the property unless agreed otherwise.
  • Cancellations and refunds
    Cancellation and refund terms depend on the property, season and booking, and are communicated to you before you confirm. Please contact us as early as possible if you need to change or cancel your booking.
  • Check-in and check-out
    Check-in is from 9:00 AM and check-out is before 12:00 noon. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability. The minimum age to check in is 18, and all adult guests may be asked to show a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in.
  • Children and extra beds
    Children are welcome. Extra beds can be arranged on request and may be charged separately. Please share the number and ages of children when you book.
  • Jungle safaris and activities
    Jungle safaris are operated by the Forest Department, not by TripTree, and are charged separately and payable on arrival. Safari timings and availability are decided by the Forest Department and may change at short notice.
  • Wildlife and guest safety
    Our properties are close to forests and wildlife. For your safety, please follow staff instructions, do not feed or approach animals, avoid walking outside lit areas after dark and keep children supervised at all times.
  • Guest conduct and property
    Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the property during their stay. Please respect the local community and environment, keep noise to a minimum at night and dispose of waste responsibly.
  • Your information
    Details you share through our contact and booking forms are used only to respond to your enquiry and manage your booking. We do not sell or share your information with third parties except where needed to fulfil your booking. To have your details removed, email us at booking@triptree.co.in.
