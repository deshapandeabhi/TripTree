About TripTree

welcome to TripTree

Nature Stays,
Thoughtfully Hosted

Holidays close to nature, made simple.
TripTree Resorts & Homestays is a Bengaluru-based collection of nature stays near some of South India's most loved wildlife destinations: Bandipur, Mudumalai and Masinagudi in the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, and Harangi in Coorg.
Each property has its own character, from thatched farmhouse cottages to forest estates, but they share the same idea: comfortable rooms, honest food, warm hosts and the wild right outside your door.
