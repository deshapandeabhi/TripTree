Mudumalai Estate

Deluxe Cottage: Rs. 2,500 / person / night

Deluxe Cottages in the Forest

Mudumalai, Nilgiris, Tamil Nadu
Mudumalai Estate offers a unique and authentic experience of local culture, as the property is owned and managed by local families. With a range of amenities and services, the estate provides a comfortable stay for travellers looking to immerse themselves in the beauty and tranquillity of the region.
Don't be surprised by an unexpected visit from the jungle's residents right at your doorstep.
